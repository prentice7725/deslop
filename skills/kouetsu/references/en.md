# English patterns

Not a banned-word list. Fix these when they repeat, and only after fixing the cause (content and clarity).

- **Nominalizations**: "perform an analysis of" → "analyze"; "the implementation of X was carried out" → "we implemented X".
- **Agentless passive**: "it was decided that" → who decided.
- **Negative parallelism**: "It's not X, it's Y", "not just X but Y" → state Y.
- **Significance claims**: "plays a crucial role", "a testament to", "marks a pivotal moment", "game-changer" → say what changes, or cut.
- **Trailing -ing clauses**: ", highlighting the importance of…", ", ensuring a seamless…" → a concrete result, or cut.
- **Signposting**: "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing", "It's worth noting that", "In conclusion" → cut.
- **Stacked hedges**: "may potentially", "could possibly help to" → one hedge, with the reason.
- **Stock vocabulary and openers**: "In today's fast-paced world", "delve", "landscape", "robust", "seamless", "leverage" → the plain word. Swapping the word alone fixes nothing if the sentence still says nothing.
- **Chat residue**: "Great question!", "I hope this helps", "Let me know if…" → cut.
- **Formulaic dash asides and title-case headings** used throughout → restructure the sentence; use sentence case.
